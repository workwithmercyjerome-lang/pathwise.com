Questions and contact.

Do I need an account?

No. You can take the assessment and browse careers without one. An account saves your results and favourite careers.

How are matches calculated?

Your answers build a profile across eight areas (such as science, numbers and creativity). Each career has its own profile, and the percentage shows how closely the two line up. It is guidance, not a test of ability.

Is this official admission advice?

No. Cut-off marks, fees and requirements change by institution and year. Confirm with the school, exam body or professional regulator.
